[[auto-editor]]
bin\auto-editor.exe  (auto-editor {{AE_VERSION}})
  A separate program. Auto-Editor GUI starts it as a command-line tool and
  doesn't link with it, so the MIT License above does not cover it, and its
  license does not extend to Auto-Editor GUI.

  It is the unmodified Windows release binary from
    https://github.com/WyattBlue/auto-editor/releases/tag/{{AE_VERSION}}
  auto-editor's own code is in the public domain (Unlicense). The binary also
  statically links FFmpeg and other libraries, including the GPL-licensed x264
  and x265 encoders, so the binary as a whole is distributed under the GNU
  General Public License, version 2 or later. A copy of the GPL is in
  bin\auto-editor.GPL-2.0.txt.

  Corresponding source code: auto-editor's source and the build scripts used to
  make this binary are at
    https://github.com/WyattBlue/auto-editor/tree/{{AE_VERSION}}
  and the sources of the libraries they build are available from their
  projects (https://ffmpeg.org, https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264,
  https://bitbucket.org/multicoreware/x265_git and the others named in those
  build scripts).

  You may delete bin\auto-editor.exe; the app can download auto-editor again
  from Settings > Tools.
